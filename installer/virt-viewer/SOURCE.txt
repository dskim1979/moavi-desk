virt-viewer for Windows (remote-viewer) — bundled with MOAVI Desk
=================================================================

This folder holds an unmodified copy of virt-viewer for Windows, unpacked from the official
installer published by the virt-viewer project:

    https://gitlab.com/virt-viewer/virt-viewer/-/releases/v11.0/downloads/virt-viewer-x64-11.0-1.0.msi

virt-viewer is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (GPLv2+).
The libraries next to it (GTK, GLib, spice-gtk, libusb, usbredir, gstreamer and others from the
MSYS2/MinGW builds) are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL) or other
open-source licences; see the files under share\ and the upstream projects.

virt-viewer is a separate program. MOAVI Desk only starts remote-viewer.exe with a connection
file; it does not link to it.
